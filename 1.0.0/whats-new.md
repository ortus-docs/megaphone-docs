# What is new in 1.1.0

Durable notifications now follow Megaphone’s existing notification, notifiable, provider, and delegate patterns. Plan and queue through NotificationService; dispatch through registered providers and typed result objects. Preferences, owned subscriptions, recovery, history, and keyset inbox pagination have public service APIs. Existing synchronous notifications and numbered cursors remain compatible.

See [durable delivery](guides/durable-delivery.md) and the [upgrade guide](upgrade-guide.md).
